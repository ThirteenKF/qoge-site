Ahead: catalysts

Events that could move ZEC up (▲) or down (▼). Expectations, not promises — dates in crypto slip. Updated Nov 18, 2026.

▲ ZCSH ETF flows. SoSoValue data every day. Mostly outflows since late September; a turn to inflows is the first sign of new demand. Sources: crypto.news — ZCSH: −$93.6M in a week ↗ · The Block — Grayscale: $1M and outflows ↗
▲ SEC decision on the WINK ETF. A second spot ETF from the Winklevosses with a 0.25% fee — 10× cheaper than ZCSH. S-1 filed Oct 6, 2026; no decision date. Sources: Unchained — Winklevoss Zcash ETF ↗
▲ Post-quantum signatures and Tachyon (2027). Team targets: January 2027 for signatures, Tachyon integration for scaling private transactions from Q1. Sources: Decrypt — the quantum-safety plan ↗
▲ Cypherpunk purchases. 341,859 ZEC in its treasury, target — 5% of supply. The company also mines and keeps everything it mines. Sources: The Block — Cypherpunk and the 5% target ↗ · ForkLog — Fortitude and Cypherpunk mining ↗
▼ The EU AMLR ban — July 1, 2027. Licensed EU exchanges and custodians may not handle ZEC; delistings may start earlier. Sources: Cointelegraph — the EU privacy-coin ban ↗
▼ Miner and dormant-wallet selling. Miners receive ≈525K ZEC a year and Fortitude sells what it mines. After Nov 11, ≈19K ZEC remain on dormant wallets. Sources: PrimeXBT — ZEC below $1,000 ↗ · Protos — the DCG miner ↗
▼ The November 2028 halving. Miner reward halves and mining cost doubles (≈$400–540), with almost no fees. The dev fund’s future must be settled before it. Sources: Bankless — the dev fund debate ↗

The bottom line

1. Growth of access, not usage. The September peak coincided with the ETF launch, Cypherpunk buying and record leverage. Network fees are ≈$0.8M a year against a ≈$15B market cap. Sources: Blockworks — Q3 2026 report ↗ · KuCoin — ZCSH inflows ↗

2. US versus EU. Optional privacy won an ETF and Coinbase listings, but from 2027 Europe still puts ZEC next to Monero. Buyers are concentrated: the ETF and Cypherpunk hold ≈5% of coins. Sources: Cointelegraph — the EU ban ↗ · The Block — Grayscale and ZCSH ↗

3. The best health signal is the shielded pool. Even after the Orchard bug, 29% of coins are shielded and more than half of transactions are private. Check in a month: ZCSH flows, the shielded share and transaction count. Sources: Blockworks — Q3 2026 report ↗ · The Block — Ironwood ↗
