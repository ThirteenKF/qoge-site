The bottom line

1. Rising on hacks is a sign of liquidity, not health. After the Binance hack RUNE rose 13% and gave it all back within two days. About $44M of stolen funds went through THORChain, and the fees on such flows are tiny: after Bybit — about $5M on $5.4B of volume. Sources: Cointelegraph — RUNE +13% ↗ · Forklog — the Chainalysis estimate ↗ · Coinranking — $5M on $5.4B ↗

2. Tokenomics became honest, but holders get little. Block rewards have been off since February 2025, ADR 023 burned 64.4M RUNE from the reserve, market cap ≈ FDV. But of the fees, 10% goes to TCY (former THORFi depositors), 20% to protocol-owned liquidity, and only 1% is burned. Sources: THORChain — tokenomics as of Oct 2025 ↗ · dev.thorchain.org — ADR 023 ↗ · dev.thorchain.org — TCY ↗ · DefiLlama — methodology (1% burn) ↗

3. Neutrality is the product and the main risk at once. The network refused three times to block stolen funds (Bybit, Bitget, Binance). Privacy coins offer a chance of “clean” growth; regulators are the threat. Check in a month: the Monero pool, volume without hacks, the node count (111 now) and the response to the FBI. Sources: Forklog — Pluto leaves (Bybit) ↗ · Forklog — no to Bitget ↗ · Coinranking — 111 nodes ↗
