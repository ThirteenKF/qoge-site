Injective (INJ), explained simply

A full breakdown of Injective: the on-chain orderbook, the real supply (122.9M INJ, not 100M) and why it still grows, burns, competitors and valuation, the institutional track — now with the first spot ETF (INJC) — and the main open question: demand.

Впереди: катализаторы

Events that could move INJ or strengthen the network. Expectations, not promises — dates in crypto slip. Updated Nov 9, 2026.

▲ INJ ETF — live. Canary Staked INJ ETF (INJC) has traded on Nasdaq since Nov 5, 2026: $16.1M in the first days (~2M INJ). Pending: 21Shares and REX-Osprey. Inflows are the main way to offset ~5.4M INJ of yearly issuance.
▲ SEC transfer agent — registered (August 2026). Lets US issuers put tokenized securities on Injective.
▲ Electro Chain — infrastructure for autonomous financial AI agents (roadmap). A bet on the x402/AP2 agent niche.
▲ KRW1 × BDACS — the won stablecoin is tied to RWA settlement: more turnover = more revenue for the BuyBack.
▲ Monthly buyback & burn — the next round at the end of the month; revenue growth directly increases burns.
▼ Risk catalysts: ETF outflows; flat revenue (~$210–270K/mo — burns ~15× below issuance); KRW1 regulatory limits; Hyperliquid equity markets (HIP-3).

Подытоживая

1. Supply is not deflationary — demand is the question. There are 122.9M INJ on-chain, not 100M, and staking issuance (~5.4M a year) is ~15× larger than burns: supply grows ~4% a year. The project's problem is not tokens but users: TVL $12.9M and open interest $2.3M against a ~$0.8–1.0B market cap.

2. The institutional road delivered a product. Meridian → Bitnomial futures (CFTC) → Robinhood → SEC transfer agent → the INJC ETF on Nasdaq. But the valuation (~250–320× annual revenue) already prices this in, while on volume Injective is at dYdX scale, not Hyperliquid's.

3. Check in a month: INJC assets and the 21Shares/REX-Osprey decisions, the next BuyBack, and the bank-module supply on Mintscan. If revenue reaches ~$3–4M a month, burns would catch up with issuance — the deflation thesis would become real.
