What the terms mean12 terms · tap to opencollapse
QUAI
The main coin of the Quai network. Everything on this page is about it.
Qi
Quai’s second coin, used for payments. It can be converted into QUAI and back.
SOAP
Quai’s buyback program: miners also mine BCH, LTC, DOGE and RVN, those coins are sold, and the money buys QUAI that is then burned.
Burn
Sending coins to an address nobody can spend from. They leave circulation for good.
Genesis unlock
Coins created at launch (team, investors, foundation) that become sellable step by step on a schedule.
Circulating supply
How many QUAI exist right now and can be traded.
Order book, depth ±2%
An exchange’s list of buy and sell orders. Depth +2% is how many dollars can be bought before the price rises 2%. Bigger depth means a calmer price.
DEX, pool, TVL
A DEX is an exchange inside the blockchain; trades go through a pool of two coins. TVL is the money locked in all the pools.
Hashference
The Quai team’s AI service; requests can be paid with mining, QUAI or Qi. More requests means real use of the network.
x402 Work
A way to pay for a request with a few seconds of computing instead of money.
pp
Percentage points: the difference between two percentages (38% vs 46% is −8 pp).
ATH
All-time high: the highest price ever.
