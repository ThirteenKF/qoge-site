The bottom line

1. The business is crypto’s #1 infrastructure. Swift, DTCC, J.P. Morgan and Schwab already work with Chainlink, 70%+ of wrapped BTC runs on CCIP, and $4B+ left LayerZero after the Kelp DAO hack. Demand for the network isn’t in doubt. Sources: Chainlink — Q3 2026 review ↗ · Chainlink — Link:NYC ↗ · KuCoin — LayerZero exodus ↗

2. The token is linked to the business only through the Reserve. The Reserve spends ≈$71M a year on LINK and covers ≈9% of unlocks. Every quarter ≈14.4M LINK goes to Binance — the main seller of LINK is the team itself. Onchain revenue is ≈$59M a year; market cap is ≈126× that. Sources: Altcoinbuzz — Reserve +1.54M in Q3 ↗ · MEXC — April unlock: 14.375M to Binance ↗ · DefiLlama — Chainlink revenue ↗

3. Watch numbers, not partnerships. From Sep 29 to Nov 14 LINK fell 37.6% amid the year’s best news. There are two turning points: a multiplied Reserve revenue or the end of unlocks around 2029. Check in a quarter: the Reserve in dollars and tokens, the unlock size, the DTCC launch. Sources: CoinGecko — LINK ↗ · Chainlink — Reserve ↗ · ChainCatcher — June 2026 unlock ↗
